Cities are growing

More than half of the world's people already live in cities, and the number keeps rising.

Cities use the most energy

Transport, buildings and waste in cities cause a large share of global carbon emissions.

Technology can help

Clean energy, smart transport and good waste systems can cut that impact. People make them work.
